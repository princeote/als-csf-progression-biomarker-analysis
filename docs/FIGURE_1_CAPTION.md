**Figure 1. Internal cross-validated model comparison for fast-versus-slow ALS progression classification.** Points show pooled held-out ROC AUC from repeated stratified 5-fold cross-validation with 100 repeats; horizontal bars show repeat-level 95% intervals across repeats. Models include the duration-free clinical baseline, the primary predefined final mechanism 7 panel, the final mechanism 7 panel plus clinical covariates, the broader 14-protein literature-supported comparator panel, the broader 14-protein panel plus clinical covariates, and the exploratory training-fold data-driven top-10 panel. Balanced and unweighted logistic-regression and random-forest variants are evaluated as class-weighting sensitivity analyses. Results are exploratory and require external validation.
